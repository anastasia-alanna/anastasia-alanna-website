Accessibility Statement

Accessibility
matters.

Anastasia Alanna is committed to making this website accessible and usable for all visitors, including people with disabilities.

We are continually working to improve the accessibility and usability of AnastasiaAlanna.com and aim to follow generally recognized accessibility practices.

Our Commitment

We believe everyone deserves equal access to information and services online. We are committed to providing a website experience that is inclusive, welcoming, and easy to navigate.

▦

Usability

We strive to ensure our website is easy to navigate and works in ways that support a wide range of abilities and technologies.

◉

Inclusivity

We aim to design with clarity, good contrast, readable fonts, and consistent structure to support a positive experience for all.

♡

Ongoing Improvement

We regularly review our website and make updates to improve accessibility and usability as best practices and technologies evolve.

Assistance

If you experience difficulty accessing any content, completing a form, or using any part of this website, please reach out to us.

Please describe the page or feature causing difficulty, and we will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or assistance you need through an alternative method.

Standards & Technology

▣

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as our standard for accessibility. Our website is built using modern web standards and optimized for modern browsers and assistive technologies.
