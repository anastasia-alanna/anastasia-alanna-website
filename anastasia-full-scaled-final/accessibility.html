Accessibility Statement

Accessibility matters.

Anastasia Alanna is committed to making this website accessible and usable for all visitors, including people with disabilities.

Last updated: May 23, 2025

Our commitment

We continually work to improve the accessibility and usability of AnastasiaAlanna.com and aim to follow generally recognized accessibility practices.

Usability

We strive to ensure the website is easy to navigate and works in ways that support a wide range of abilities, browsers, and technologies.

Standards

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, WCAG 2.1 Level AA, where reasonably possible.

Feedback and assistance

If you experience difficulty accessing content, completing a form, or using any part of this website, please contact us at hello@AnastasiaAlanna.com. Please describe the page or feature causing difficulty, and we will make reasonable efforts to provide the information through an alternative method.
