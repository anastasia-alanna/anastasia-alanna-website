Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

Anastasia Alanna respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information thoughtfully and responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit AnastasiaAlanna.com, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

Effective Date: May 23, 2025   |   Last Updated: May 23, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide
We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you inquire about our services, schedule a discovery call, or communicate with us. This may include your name, business name, email address, phone number, website, service of interest, and information about your business and goals.

Information Collected Automatically
When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on the site, referring website, and other analytics data through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected may be used to respond to inquiries, schedule and conduct discovery calls, evaluate our services, communicate with you, improve our website and services, and for legal, accounting, or security purposes.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with trusted service providers who help us operate our website and business, such as website hosting, analytics, scheduling, email, and other business tools. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

4. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience, remember preferences, and understand how our site is used. You can adjust your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality.

5. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites or use third-party tools. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites or services. Please review their privacy policies for more information.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may contact us to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information or to stop receiving marketing emails.

Email us at hello@anastasiaalanna.com to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

9. Email Communications

If you subscribe to our emails, you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting us directly. You may still receive non-promotional emails related to our business relationship.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it.

11. Visitors Outside the United States

Our website is based in the United States. If you access it from another country, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the effective date.

13. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us.
