My Story

I started Anastasia Alanna during one of the most challenging seasons of my life—going through a divorce and becoming a single mom. I needed flexibility to support my family and be present for my children.

What began as a way to create stability for my family became a calling. I realized the behind-the-scenes work that overwhelms business owners is exactly what I love to do—and what I’m truly good at.
